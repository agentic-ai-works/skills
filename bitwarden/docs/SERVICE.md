# Paid setup service (draft)

For people who want it installed and checked, not just downloaded.

**Setup (fixed price)**: remote session, install, Bitwarden login, tick-box permissions chosen with you, test with a throwaway entry, short written summary of what the agent can and cannot do.
**Hardening review**: check your agent setup (what else on the machine can reach the vault, X11 vs Wayland, backups of the vault, 2FA on the Bitwarden account itself).
**Retainer**: updates when `bw` or your distro changes, help with new agents.

Prices: set by the service provider (suggest a fixed fee per setup; no secrets are ever shared with the provider: you type the master password yourself).
